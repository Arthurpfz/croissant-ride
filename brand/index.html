Croissant Ride — brand

Dev-only reference for the design system (roadmap N16). Tokens live in src/tokens.css, component looks in src/style.css, canvas colours come from src/tokens.ts. Everything below is rendered with the real stylesheet.

Logo

A TT wheel set, deep-section front wheel and disc rear, in the Graphite colourway: slate tile, bone wheels, crust hubs. Lives in public/logo.svg; used as the favicon and on the start screen.

Croissant Ride

Name

Always Croissant Ride: two words, both capitalised, never "CroissantRide", "Croissant-Ride" or "CR". The wordmark is Geist bold with tight tracking, bone on charcoal, charcoal on crust.

Voice

A calm friend on the wheel next to you. Short, warm, never shouty. The rider is working hard and glancing for half a second, so every word must earn its place.

  • Live cues: 2–4 words, verb first. One leading symbol at most (▲ ▼ ⟳ ⚡) so the cue reads before the words do.
  • Sentence case. No ALL CAPS shouting; caps only for tiny labels like TARGET.
  • Encourage, never blame. Say what to do, not what went wrong with the rider.
  • Errors say what happened and what happens next. "Trainer lost — reconnecting…".
  • No other emoji in live cues. One is fine for a finish or celebration banner (🎉).
  • Food puns: rare, never mid-effort. Fine on empty states or the ride summary, never in cues or errors.
  • Our own words. Don't reuse cue phrases from other training apps.
  • Units: number, space, unit: 131 W, 20 min, 3 of 6.

Readability over any world

The HUD never re-tints with the scene (N9 world packs may change the world, not the HUD). Same panel over the brightest sky, the default sky, a night scene and a busy pattern. Rule: text and buttons sit on a panel or card. Exception: the cue and the banner are plain text with --shadow-text, to keep the view clean.

Contrast (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Computed live from the tokens: panel alpha composited over pure white (worst bright sky), the default sky and pure black. AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (≥24px, or ≥18.7px bold).

All rows pass AA with panels at 80% opacity (Graphite, 2026-11-01). On panels, Ash is lifted to --ash-hud and --danger is a lifted crust, both to stay above 4.5:1 over a white sky.
